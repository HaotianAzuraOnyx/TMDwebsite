Our Services 我们的服务

Complete Dental Examination全面口腔检查

A comprehensive dental examination allows our dental team to assess your teeth, gums and overall oral health. Based on our findings, we can discuss your needs and develop a personalized treatment plan to help maintain and improve your oral health.全面口腔检查可以帮助我们的牙科团队评估您的牙齿、牙龈及整体口腔健康状况。根据检查结果，我们会与您讨论您的口腔健康需求，并制定适合您的个性化治疗计划。

Dental Cleaning洗牙与洁治

Professional dental cleanings help remove plaque, tartar and buildup to keep your teeth and gums healthy. We accept dental insurance. Contact us today to book your cleaning or ask about your insurance coverage.专业洗牙可以帮助清除牙菌斑、牙结石及牙齿表面的沉积物，帮助保持牙齿和牙龈健康。我们接受牙科保险. 欢迎联系我们预约洗牙或咨询牙科保险相关信息。

Dental Fillings补牙

Dental fillings are used to repair teeth affected by cavities, minor fractures or other damage. Tooth-coloured restorative materials can help restore the function and appearance of the tooth while preserving as much healthy tooth structure as possible.补牙主要用于修复因蛀牙、轻微断裂或其他原因受损的牙齿。牙色修复材料可以帮助恢复牙齿的功能和外观，同时尽可能保留健康的天然牙体组织。

Crowns & BridgesCrowns & Bridges · 牙冠与牙桥

Dental crowns can protect and restore weakened or significantly damaged teeth, while bridges can be used to replace certain missing teeth. Our dental team can assess your needs and discuss appropriate restorative options for improving function and appearance.牙冠可以保护并修复较为脆弱或严重受损的牙齿，而牙桥则可用于替代部分缺失的牙齿。我们的牙科团队会根据您的具体情况，为您介绍合适的修复方案，以改善牙齿功能和外观。

Tooth Extractions & Root Canal Treatment拔牙与根管治疗

When a tooth is damaged, severely decayed, or infected, our dental team will assess the condition and discuss the most appropriate treatment options with you. Whenever possible, root canal treatment can help save and preserve your natural tooth by removing infected or inflamed tissue from inside the tooth and sealing it to prevent further infection.当牙齿出现严重蛀坏、损伤或感染时，我们的牙科团队会仔细评估您的情况，并与您讨论适合的治疗方案。在条件允许的情况下，根管治疗可以帮助保留您的天然牙齿，通过清除牙齿内部发炎或感染的组织，并进行清洁和封闭，以防止进一步感染。

When a tooth cannot be predictably restored or saved, tooth extraction may be recommended. Our team will explain your options and help make your treatment as comfortable as possible.如果牙齿已经无法有效修复或保留，则可能需要进行拔牙治疗。我们的团队会详细为您解释治疗选择，并尽可能为您提供舒适、安心的治疗体验。

Orthodontics & Invisalign®牙齿矫正与隐适美

A straighter smile can improve both your confidence and oral health. At Toronto Maple Dental, we offer orthodontic treatment options for patients looking to improve the alignment of their teeth and bite. Our team will assess your individual needs and discuss treatment options that are appropriate for you.想拥有更整齐、自信的笑容？多伦多枫华牙科提供牙齿矫正服务，帮助改善牙齿排列及咬合问题。我们的牙科团队会根据您的牙齿及咬合情况进行评估，并与您讨论适合您的矫正治疗方案。

For patients looking for a more discreet alternative to traditional braces, Invisalign® clear aligners use a series of customized, removable aligners to gradually move your teeth into their desired positions. Invisalign aligners are nearly invisible and can be removed for eating, brushing and flossing, making them a convenient option for many teens and adults.对于希望选择比传统牙套更加隐形、美观的患者，Invisalign® 隐适美透明牙套通过一系列个性化定制的可摘式透明牙套，逐步将牙齿移动到理想位置。隐适美牙套近乎透明，并可在进食、刷牙及使用牙线时取下，为许多青少年及成人提供方便、灵活的矫正选择。
